- (D) Hexadecimal uses less computer memory than Binary.

### Character Coding Systems Summary (අක්ෂර කේතන පද්ධති සාරාංශය)

| **BCD** | **4 Bits** per digit | 10 digits (`0000` to `1001`) | Encodes decimal numbers 0–9 individually. |

| **Extended ASCII** | **8 Bits** | $2^8 = 256$ characters | English + basic European special symbols. |
